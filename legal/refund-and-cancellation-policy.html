This policy explains how cancellations and refunds work for Cairn. Payments are processed by Dodo Payments, our Merchant of Record, which handles billing, tax, and refund processing on our behalf; approved refunds are returned to your original payment method, and the corresponding tax is reversed automatically.

The essentials

  • The free Cairn desktop app is free — there is nothing to refund.
  • Cairn Pro is billed monthly or annually. You subscribe deliberately; there are no auto-charging free trials.

Cancellation

You can cancel your Cairn Pro subscription at any time from Settings → Billing in the app. Cancellation stops all future billing and stops cloud sync at the end of your current paid period — you keep Pro access until then. Cancelling never deletes your local data, which remains on your device. Your encrypted server-side vault is retained for 90 days after cancellation and then permanently crypto-shredded.

Refunds

  • 14-day goodwill refund (new subscriptions). If Cairn Pro isn't right for you, contact support@[yourdomain] within 14 days of your first payment and we will issue a full refund of that payment.
  • Statutory rights. Where local law grants you a stronger right of withdrawal or refund (for example, EU/UK consumer law or applicable Indian consumer-protection rules), those rights apply and are not limited by this policy.
  • Renewals. Renewal charges are generally non-refundable once the new billing period has begun, except where required by law or granted at our discretion for good cause (for example, a prolonged outage that prevented use). Cancel before a renewal date to avoid the next charge.
  • Annual plans. If you cancel mid-term, access continues until the end of the paid year. Partial-year refunds are at our discretion unless required by law.

How to request a refund

Email support@[yourdomain] from your account email address, including your approximate purchase date. We (via Dodo Payments) will process approved refunds to your original payment method; they typically take a few business days to appear on your statement.

Chargebacks

If something is wrong, please contact us first — we can almost always resolve it faster than a chargeback. Fraudulent or bad-faith chargebacks may result in suspension of your account.
