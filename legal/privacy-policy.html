Cairn is a discipline-first trading journal. This policy explains what data we process and — just as importantly — what we cannot access, because Cairn is built so that we never hold the keys to your trading data.

The short version. Cairn is local-first: your trades are stored on your own device and the app works fully offline with no account. If you enable optional cloud sync, your data is end-to-end encrypted on your device before it leaves — our servers store only ciphertext they cannot read. We do not sell your data, we run no ads, and any telemetry is off by default.

1. What we collect, and why

If you use only the free desktop app (no account): we collect nothing. Your data never leaves your device unless you choose to move it (for example, into a synced folder you control).

2. What we can never access

Your vault content — your trades, journal entries, and account details — is encrypted on your device. The encryption key is derived from your password (via Argon2id) and never leaves your device unencrypted. On our servers we hold only ciphertext and a data key wrapped under a key we do not possess. There is no admin override and no backdoor. If you lose both your password and your recovery phrase, we cannot recover your synced data — this is by design.

The web app uses a single essential, secure, http-only session cookie (__Host-refresh) required to keep you signed in; no consent is required for strictly essential cookies. 4. Who we share data with (sub-processors)

We share data only with the service providers strictly needed to run the paid service. Each is bound by a data-processing agreement. The current list is maintained at our sub-processors page and typically includes: our API hosting provider, our managed database provider, our transactional email provider (Resend), our payment Merchant of Record (Dodo Payments), and our error-tracking provider. The free local app uses none of these. We do not share your data with anyone else, and we never sell it.

5. Retention

  • Local data: stays on your device until you delete it. Cancelling a subscription never deletes it.
  • Synced (encrypted) vault: retained while your subscription is active; after cancellation it is kept for 90 days, then crypto-shredded (we delete the wrapped key, permanently rendering the ciphertext undecryptable).
  • Account & billing records: retained as required for legal and tax obligations, then deleted.
  • Operational logs: retained for [30–90] days.

8. Security

We use end-to-end encryption for synced vault content (we cannot decrypt it), Argon2id password hashing with a server-side pepper, TLS-only transport, short-lived access tokens with rotating refresh tokens and reuse detection, rate limiting, and strict security headers. No system is perfectly secure, but we design to minimise what an attacker — or we ourselves — could ever access.
