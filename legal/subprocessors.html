Cairn uses the following sub-processors to provide the paid cloud service. Each is bound by a data-processing agreement (DPA). The free local desktop app uses none of these — it runs entirely on your device. Because our hosting and database providers only ever hold your vault as ciphertext, neither they nor we can read your trading data.
