The Core Idea

Trade through Cairn, not after it

Trading failure is ~80% discipline, ~20% knowledge. You know how to take a good trade — you lack the system that stops the bad ones. Cairn solves one pattern: "I know the rule. I break the rule. I lose. I promise to follow it. I break it again."

First Run

Onboarding — from install to first trade

Cairn sets up your trading identity once, then gets out of the way.

1

Open the app

Cairn launches as a desktop app (Windows / Mac / Linux). No login required — it works fully offline from the first second.

2

Create your first account

Add a trading account: name it, set starting balance, currency, and leverage (default 100:1). Mark it as a personal or prop/firm account with its own configurable rule set — no prop-firm names are hardcoded.

3

Set your rules

Define the rules you keep breaking: daily trade limit, max daily loss circuit breaker, risk % per trade, R-target alerts, allowed killzone hours, and your instrument list (19 defaults included).

4

Pick your timezone

Defaults to America/New_York so killzones line up with the ICT session model. Fully changeable in Settings.

5

Build your playbook

Add your setups (the patterns you actually trade) so each trade can be tagged and later graded by setup.

6

You're live

The dashboard now shows your balance, discipline ring, and streaks. Open the New Trade panel and the cockpit takes over.

What Cairn Does Today

Capabilities so far

Everything below is built and shipping in the desktop app (v1.1 + v1.2, Waves 0–3 complete).

🎯 Pre-Trade Gate

Before you place a trade, Cairn runs the rule engine: daily trade limit, max-loss circuit breaker, risk size, killzone timing. Violations are blocked or require a typed acknowledgment. Hard locks can't be overridden.

🧮 Risk Calculator

Enter risk in $ or % and Cairn auto-computes lot size using your account's leverage and the instrument's pip value — money math in decimal types, correct to the cent/pip.

📝 Fast Trade Logging

Round-trip logging under ~90 seconds. Remember-last-trade, invalidation chips, one-tap emotion, and clean-close shortcuts remove busywork while keeping honesty fields intact.

🧠 Honesty Forcing Fields

Invalidation, emotional state, and rules-broken are structured so they can't be silently skipped or defaulted to "N/A". Self-awareness is required, not optional.

✂️ Partial Closes & Screenshots

Scale out with partial exits (% based), attach an optional chart screenshot to any trade. Screenshots never block submission.

🗓️ Calendar & Trade Log

A P&L calendar view plus a full searchable trade log. Activate drafts, review any past trade, export a trade review to PDF.

📊 Analytics Suite

Six analytics tabs with a shared filter bar: performance, adherence, behavioral patterns, setups, and more — built on your real, honest data.

💡 Local Insight Engine

On-device statistical heuristics surface findings like "win-rate drops when urgency > 7", tilt-cycle detection, and expectancy-by-setup — no LLM, no network, no telemetry.

🏅 Composite Score & A–F Grade

Every trade and session gets a discipline composite score and letter grade. Rules-broken are auto-detected from your data, not self-reported.

📓 Notebook & Playbooks

A free-form notebook for reflection and structured playbooks for your setups, so review is connected to the patterns you trade.

📥 Statement Import

Import trade history from MT5, cTrader, and TradingView HTML/CSV statements. A parser + de-duper eliminates double entry — free, no paid data feeds.

💾 Backup & Self-Healing

Local SQLite is the canonical store with a backup service and self-healing checks. Point it at any synced folder for cloud-style backup, no APIs required.

Design language: glassmorphism with depth — frosted surfaces, layered glow, and physics-based motion. The calm UI is itself a discipline feature. Voice is a respected mentor: direct, calm, never cutesy or alarmist.

Step by Step

How to use Cairn

A day in the cockpit, from market open to weekly review.

1

Set your session bias

Open the dashboard and log your bias for the session. This frames every trade you take today.

2

Size the trade in the calculator

In the New Trade panel, type your entry, stop, and risk ($ or %). Cairn returns the exact lot size — no mental math, no float errors.

3

Pass the gate

Hit submit and the rule engine checks you in under 20 seconds. If you're inside your rules: trade clean. If not: Cairn blocks it, or makes you type an acknowledgment, or hard-locks the session if a circuit breaker tripped.

4

Manage & close

Scale out with partial closes, attach a screenshot if you want. On close, the mechanical fields fill fast and the trade drops into your reflection queue.

5

Reflect on the Review screen

Later — not mid-trade — work through the deferred queue: invalidation, emotion, what you'd change. Honest reflection without front-loading it onto the close click.

6

Import what you traded elsewhere

Drop in your MT5 / cTrader / TradingView statement. Cairn parses and de-dupes so your journal is complete without double entry.

7

Read the insights weekly

Open Analytics. The local insight engine tells you where discipline slips — by setup, by emotion, by time of day. Check your A–F grade and composite score trend.

8

Adjust your rules

Every preference is configurable in Settings. Tighten the rules that the data shows you keep breaking. Next week, the gate enforces the better version of you.
