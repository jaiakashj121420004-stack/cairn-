## The essentials

- **Free tier:** the local desktop app is free — nothing to refund.
- **Cairn Pro:** billed monthly or annually. You subscribe deliberately; there are no
  auto-charging free trials.

## Refunds

- **14-day goodwill window (new subscriptions):** if Cairn Pro isn't right for you, contact
  support@cairn.app within 14 days of your **first** payment for a full refund of that
  payment. [Confirm this matches your processor config and statutory minimums.]
- **Statutory rights:** where local law grants a stronger right of withdrawal or refund
  (e.g. EU/UK consumer law, India consumer protection), those rights apply and are not limited
  by this policy.
- **Renewals:** renewal charges are generally non-refundable once the new period has started,
  except where required by law or at our discretion for good cause (e.g. an outage that
  prevented use). You can cancel before a renewal to avoid the next charge.
- **Annual plans:** if you cancel mid-term, access continues until the end of the paid year;
  partial-year refunds are at our discretion unless required by law.

## How to request

Email **support@cairn.app** from your account email with your approximate purchase date.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method by the payment processor and may take a few
business days to appear.

## Cancelling

Cancel any time from Settings → Billing. Cancellation stops future billing and sync at the end
of the paid period. **Your local data is never deleted by cancelling.**

## Chargebacks

If something's wrong, please email us first — we can usually resolve it faster than a
chargeback. Fraudulent chargebacks may result in account suspension.
